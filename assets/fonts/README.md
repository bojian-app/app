AnnotationUI is a renamed, regular-weight subset of Noto Sans SC from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc, under the bundled SIL Open Font License. ResumeDocument, applied-template previews, template marketplace previews, and PDF exports use Bojian Resume Sans (`resume-sans-regular.ttf`, `resume-sans-medium.ttf`, `resume-sans-bold.ttf`): static Regular (400), Medium (500/600) and Bold (700) instances of the Noto Sans SC variable font from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc, under the bundled SIL Open Font License (`OFL.txt`). As the OFL requires for modified versions, the fonts are renamed and do not use the reserved name "Source". Each face is subsetted to GB2312 (6763 Han characters and its symbols), ASCII, Latin-1 and Latin Extended-A, common punctuation, symbol and fullwidth blocks, and every character in the app sources and bundled sample data; Flutter falls back to system fonts for anything else, and PDF export reports characters the font lacks. U+00B7 and U+2022 map to new proportional glyphs (357 and 308 units wide) instead of the font's fullwidth dot. PDF embeds used glyph subsets; Word names `微软雅黑` and embeds no font.

Rebuild from the app root with fontTools: `python3 tool/fonts/make_resume_sans.py 'NotoSansSC[wght].ttf' assets/fonts`. Rerun it when new interface text adds characters outside the set.
